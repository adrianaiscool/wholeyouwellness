Home Workouts

NO EQUIPMENT. NO EXCUSES. JUST RESULTS.

Why Work Out at Home?

You don't need a gym membership to get in great shape. Bodyweight training is incredibly effective for building strength, improving cardiovascular health, and increasing flexibility — all from the comfort of your living room. These workouts are perfect for beginners, busy schedules, or anyone who prefers to train on their own terms.

Foundational Bodyweight Exercises

  • Push-ups — Builds chest, shoulders, and triceps. Modify by doing them on your knees.
  • Bodyweight Squats — Strengthens legs and glutes with no equipment needed.
  • Plank — Core stability exercise. Hold for 20–60 seconds.
  • Glute Bridges — Activates glutes and lower back. Great for beginners.
  • Mountain Climbers — Full-body cardio and core exercise combined.
  • Jumping Jacks — Simple cardio warm-up to get your heart rate up.

4-Week Home Workout Plan

Day Workout Duration Intensity
Monday Full Body Strength (Push-ups, Squats, Plank) 25 min Moderate
Tuesday Cardio Blast (Jumping Jacks, Mountain Climbers, Burpees) 20 min High
Wednesday Core & Flexibility (Plank holds, Bridges, Stretching) 20 min Low
Thursday Lower Body Focus (Squats, Lunges, Glute Bridges) 25 min Moderate
Friday Upper Body & Core (Push-ups, Dips, Plank variations) 25 min Moderate
Saturday Active Recovery (Walk, light yoga, or stretching) 30 min Very Low
Sunday Rest — —

Tips for Working Out at Home

  • Set a consistent workout time — treating it like an appointment makes it easier to stick to.
  • Clear a small space in your home and keep it dedicated to workouts.
  • Start with 3 days per week and build from there.
  • Track your reps and progress in a notebook or on your phone.
  • Invest in a yoga mat — it makes floor exercises much more comfortable.
